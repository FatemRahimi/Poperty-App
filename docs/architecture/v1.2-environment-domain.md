# Environment Domain Envelope

**Domain version:** `environment-domain-1.0.0`  
**Status:** Live adapter over existing Environment Agency Flood Map for Planning evidence.  
**wiredIntoLiveAnalysis:** `true` (with PLANNING).  
**Scope:** flood evidence only.

This domain answers: **what flood-zone evidence do we currently have from the configured EA dataset?**  
It does not score environmental risk, predict flooding, adjust valuation, or infer insurance or legal outcomes.

## Boundary

Owns: existing Flood Map for Planning (rivers and sea) Zone 2 / Zone 3 point-in-polygon evidence, provenance, coverage/unavailable semantics.

Does not own: surface water, reservoirs, climate projections, contamination, radon, subsidence, coastal erosion, air quality, noise, ground stability, mining, water quality, biodiversity, energy/carbon, insurance, mortgageability, valuation impact, legal liability.

## Canonical vs legacy

- **Canonical going forward:** `report.environmentDomain` (kernel envelope).
- **Legacy consumer field:** `propertyFacts.facts.flood` — same evidence, unchanged shape.
- Both are derived from one `getFloodEvidence` result. The adapter does not re-query EA.

## Dataset semantics

The integrated product is Flood Map for Planning, rivers and sea only (FeatureServer layers: Zone 3 = layer 1, Zone 2 = layer 2). England coverage. Point-in-polygon at subject coordinates — not a UPRN building footprint.

- Intersection of Zone 2 or Zone 3 is sourced FACT evidence.
- No Zone 2/3 intersection is **not** Flood Zone 1 and is **not** “no flood risk”.
- Absence of intersection only means the configured query did not return Zone 2 or Zone 3 at those coordinates.

## Registry

ENVIRONMENT is the second specialised domain with `wiredIntoLiveAnalysis: true`.  
PLANNING remains live. Other future domains remain unwired.  
Live domains are composed additively; one failure does not erase the other.
