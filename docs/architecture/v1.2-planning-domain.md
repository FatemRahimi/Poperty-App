# Planning Domain Envelope

**Domain version:** `planning-domain-1.0.0`  
**Status:** Live adapter over existing MHCLG planning-application evidence.  
**wiredIntoLiveAnalysis:** `true` (PLANNING and ENVIRONMENT are the live specialised domains).

This domain answers: **what planning evidence do we currently have?**  
It does not predict permission, score risk, or change valuation/finance/PD/DI.

## Boundary

Owns: planning applications, references, sourced status/decision/dates, subject vs nearby relationship, provenance.

Does not own: conservation areas, Article 4, listed buildings, legal title, development potential, permitted development, valuation.

## Canonical vs legacy

- **Canonical going forward:** `report.planningDomain` (kernel envelope).
- **Legacy consumer field:** `propertyFacts.facts.planning` — same evidence, unchanged shape.
- Both are derived from one `getPlanningEvidence` result. No second fetch.

## 400m

Existing area-context search radius. Not a legal or planning boundary. Unchanged this phase.

## Registry

PLANNING remains a live specialised domain with `wiredIntoLiveAnalysis: true`.  
ENVIRONMENT is now also live. Other future domains remain unwired. Live domains are composed additively; one failure does not erase the other.
