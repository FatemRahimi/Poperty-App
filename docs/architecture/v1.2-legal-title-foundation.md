# Legal & Title Evidence Foundation

**Domain version:** `legal-title-domain-0.1.0`  
**Status:** Foundation contracts + completeness adapter.  
**wiredIntoLiveAnalysis:** `false`

This phase answers: **what legal/title evidence do we actually have?**  
It does not answer whether title is good, whether a covenant binds, or whether to buy.

## Boundary

May eventually own: title identity, tenure evidence, proprietor, lease, register/plan, covenants, easements, charges, restrictions, document completeness, conflicts.

This version owns only: title-reference contract, document contract, missing-document semantics, conflict representation, adjacent tenure/lease facts, completeness findings.

Does **not** own: listed building, conservation area, Article 4, planning applications, flood, sold prices, EPC occupancy tenure.

## Safety

`UNKNOWN ≠ CLEAR`. Missing register ≠ clean title. Missing lease ≠ freehold. Missing document ≠ no restriction.

PROPERTY ≠ TITLE. UPRN ≠ title number. Retrieval date ≠ legal effective date.

Private user documents never become shared property evidence.
