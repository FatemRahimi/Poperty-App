# Building & Condition Intelligence Foundation

**Domain version:** `building-condition-domain-0.1.0`  
**Status:** Foundation contracts + completeness adapter.  
**wiredIntoLiveAnalysis:** `false`  
**Live condition assessment:** **NOT YET AVAILABLE**

This phase answers: **what physical evidence exists, and what was actually inspected?**  
It does not survey, diagnose, cost, or adjust valuation.

## Availability

| Evidence | Status |
|---|---|
| Professional condition / RICS / structural survey | **NOT AVAILABLE** |
| Observed defect records | **NOT AVAILABLE** |
| EPC rating/score | Available — **ENERGY_EVIDENCE**, not condition |
| Floor plan upload | Available — layout artefact, not condition |
| Listing photographs | Available — marketing, not inspection |
| `buildRisks` | Live heuristic — **LEGACY_HEURISTIC**, not canonical condition |

## Safety

`NOT OBSERVED ≠ GOOD CONDITION`  
`NO DEFECT RECORDED ≠ NO DEFECT EXISTS`  
`NO SURVEY ≠ STRUCTURALLY SOUND`  
`OLD EPC ≠ CURRENT BUILDING CONDITION`  
`PHOTO ≠ PROFESSIONAL INSPECTION`

Condition vocabulary is **SOURCE_NATIVE / UNKNOWN**. GOOD/FAIR/POOR are not invented because the repository has no evidence that supports them.

## Entity and components

`PROPERTY ≠ BUILDING ≠ UNIT`.  
Component refs (`ROOF`, `HEATING`, `UNKNOWN`, …) do not imply inspection.

## Boundaries

- **EPC** — energy evidence only. Listing-asserted ratings already exist. Official MHCLG EPB open data is the selected next source (`decisions/physical-property-identity-source.md`) and is **NOT_IMPLEMENTED**. Official certificates still must not become a condition survey.  
- **Floor plan** — layout artefact only  
- **Photo** — may be an evidence artefact; no visual diagnosis  
- **Survey documents** — separate from legal title documents (`BUILDING_DOCUMENT_TYPE`)  
- **MAINTENANCE_CAPEX** — future; answers cost of intervention, not observation  
- **PROJECT_COST** — future; no £/sqm or replacement estimates here
