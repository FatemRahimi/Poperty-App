About this property

  • The property comprises a high yielding mixed use investment fully let to a single tenant. Arranged as a ground floor and basement retail unit with a single residential dwelling to the first and second floors, the property is fully let to a private individual trading as Hair House Hairdressers on a recently signed five year lease expiring 13.11.2029 and paying £10,250 per annum. The lease includes a rent review in the third year, which is index linked with no cap or collar. The property occupies a prominent pitch with a frontage to Chapel Street, close to the junction of Main Street. The street is the main thoroughfare from several town centre car parks with an approx. 135 spaces as well as Bingley Station which is immediately to the rear of the property. The 5 Rise Shopping Centre is opposite and nearby tenants include Home Bargains, Co-Op, Boots and Subway.
